Capstone Format Revision

Why the ZPPSU CICS IT/IS capstone outline changed, how the new TRB-approved structure differs chapter by chapter, and a complete worked sample — the TDFOSIAN academe–employer portal.

Why the Format Changed

A format built for consistency, not just compliance.

The Problem

The previous outline satisfied CMO 25 s. 2015 only at the chapter level. Subsections, literature scope, research instruments and ethics were undefined — producing inconsistent manuscripts across panels.

The Reform

The Technical Review Board (TRB) reformulated the outline so every chapter's expectations are explicit for both BSIT and BSIS capstone groups.

Numbered Subsections

Every chapter now carries decimal-numbered subsections (1.1, 2.3, 3.1.2…) removing ambiguity on what each section must contain.

Explicit RRL Scope

Local vs. foreign literature and systems are separate required subsections, plus a mandatory synthesis table reconciling them.

SDLC-Anchored Methodology

Chapter III is built around the group's chosen SDLC model, with ethics and statistical tools formally required.

Outcome-Framed Results

Chapter IV is restructured around the study's specific objectives rather than a development narrative.

2

Previous Format — At a Glance

No decimal numbering; subsection depth and RRL/methodology scope were left to each adviser's discretion.

Chapter 1 — Background of the Problem

Introduction · Project Context · Purpose · Objectives · Scope & Limitations · Definition of Terms

Chapter 2 — RRL & Systems

Technical Background (single, undivided section)

Chapter 3 — Design & Methodology

Requirements Analysis · Requirements Documentation · System/Product Design

Chapter 4 — Development & Testing

Prototype Description · Development · Testing · Implementation Plan

Chapter 5 — Results, Conclusion…

Implementation results, discussions, conclusion and recommendations combined into one chapter.

References

ACM format · Appendices as listed.

3

New Format — TRB Approved

Decimal-numbered subsections throughout; methodology explicitly anchored to the chosen SDLC model.

Ch. 1 — Introduction

1.1 Background · 1.2 Project Context · 1.3 Purpose & Description · 1.4 Objectives · 1.5 Significance · 1.6 Scope & Limitations · 1.7 Definition of Terms

Ch. 2 — Related Literature, Studies & Systems

2.1 Literature (Local) · 2.2 Literature (Foreign) · 2.3 Studies & Systems (Local) · 2.4 Studies & Systems (Foreign) · 2.5 Synthesis — Table of Comparison + Synthesis Tables

Ch. 3 — Methodology

3.1 SDLC Model (Planning→Maintenance) incl. 3.1.3.1 Technical Background · 3.2 Research Instruments · 3.3 Statistical Tools · 3.4 Ethical Considerations

Ch. 4 — Results & Discussion

4.1, 4.2, 4.3… organized per Specific Objective.

Ch. 5 — Conclusion & Recommendations

Separated from results reporting into its own focused chapter.

References

APA format · Appendices as listed (unchanged).

Structural Comparison

Previous format ○ versus ✓ new TRB-approved structure — side by side.

Chapter II — Review of Related Literature

Previous

  • Technical Background (single undivided section)

New — TRB Approved

  • 2.1 Related Literature (Local)
  • 2.2 Related Literature (Foreign)
  • 2.3 Related Studies and Systems (Local)
  • 2.4 Related Studies and Systems (Foreign)
  • 2.5 Synthesis — Comparison & Synthesis Table — NEW
Local/foreign literature and systems must now be reviewed separately and reconciled in a required synthesis table.

Chapter III — Design & Methodology

Previous

  • Requirements Analysis
  • Requirements Documentation
  • Design of Software Systems, Product and/or Processes

New — TRB Approved

  • 3.1 SDLC Model (e.g., Agile): Planning → Analysis → Design → Implementation → Testing & Integration → Maintenance
  • 3.1.3.1 Technical Background
  • 3.2 Capstone/Research Instruments
  • 3.3 Statistical Tools
  • 3.4 Ethical Considerations — NEW
Methodology is now explicitly anchored to the group's chosen SDLC model; ethics is a required, citable subsection.

Chapters IV–V — From Prototype to Outcomes

Previous

  • Ch. IV: Prototype Description, Development, Testing, Implementation Plan
  • Ch. V: Implementation Results, Discussions, Conclusion & Recommendations — combined

New — TRB Approved

  • Ch. IV: Results and Discussion — organized per Specific Objective (4.1, 4.2, 4.3…)
  • Ch. V: Conclusion and Recommendations — a separate, focused chapter
Reporting shifts from a development narrative to evidence organized around each specific objective; conclusions get their own chapter.
5

What Stays the Same

Continuity items carried over unchanged from the previous format.

References — APA

APA citation format required — unchanged in both versions.

Appendices

Source code · evaluation tools/test documents · sample I/O reports · user's guide · process/data/information flow · screen layouts · test results · sample outputs · photo documentation · CV per member · MOA (optional).

Front Matter

Title page · Executive Summary (250–300 words) · Table of Contents · List of Figures/Tables/Notations — the new format only formalizes the Executive Summary word count.

Sample Capstone — TDFOSIAN

A complete worked example of the new format: TDFOSIAN, an academe–employer portal for ZPPSU graduates.

Chapter I — Introduction

1.1 BackgroundGraduate tracer data across Zamboanga Peninsula HEIs shows persistent skills–employer mismatch, with no shared region-specific channel linking academe and industry.
1.2 Project ContextNational job boards and PESO listings are not integrated with ZPPSU's tracer study or curriculum data — employability insight stays fragmented.
1.3 Purpose & DescriptionTDFOSIAN is a web portal connecting ZPPSU academe, regional employers and job-seeking alumni through one shared platform.
1.4 ObjectivesA portal with alumni/employer registration, job matching, and a tracer-linked employability dashboard for GATEU.
1.5 SignificanceAlumni gain job access; employers gain a localized talent pool; ZPPSU gains CHED-aligned tracer and employability reporting.
1.6 Scope & LimitationsWeb-based; covers ZPPSU BSIT/BSIS/related graduates and employers registered within the Zamboanga Peninsula.
1.7 Definition of TermsTDFOSIAN · Tracer Study · Employability · cNPS · Applicant Tracking — operationally defined for the study.

References remain APA-formatted; appendices carry the same required set (source code, ISO 25010 tool, I/O reports, user's guide, ERD/data flow, screen layouts, test results, photos, CVs).

Key Takeaways

What every BSIT/BSIS capstone group must remember under the new format.

Number everything

Use decimal subsections in all chapters — panels now expect exact placement.

Split your RRL

Local ≠ foreign, literature ≠ systems — four required sections plus a synthesis table.

Name your SDLC

Chapter III walks your model phase by phase, with instruments, statistics and ethics cited.

Report per objective

Chapter IV answers each specific objective with evidence; Chapter V concludes separately.
